### Workbook logic and mechanical repairs to propose

1. **Geography:** The 120-mile radius and road miles are mixed. Preserve radial distance as the eligibility screen and road mileage/travel time as logistics, subject to owner confirmation of any policy change. Split Caldwell, Price's and McLaughlin's into individual candidate rows; Rated Candidates A17/D17 combines them under a distance that hides meaningful differences. Reconcile old Pikeville references with Durham.

2. **Scoring:** "No enforcement found" must not become verified good performance. Unknown access is not demonstrated access. Employee counts, family ownership and retail hours do not establish slaughter throughput or willingness to host. Review the evidence behind high welfare, relationship, anatomy and access scores and identify unsupported changes. Separate qualification status from comparative attractiveness.

3. **Volume:** Historical inspection bands and 2025 statewide slaughter totals are historical evidence, not present weekly plant capacity or eligible pigs available to us. Preserve units, period, species coverage and federal/state scope. [Official 2025 livestock slaughter summary](https://www.nass.usda.gov/Publications/Todays_Reports/reports/lsan0426.pdf). Avoid converting aggregate annual totals into universal current weekly ceilings.

4. **Anatomy and animal class:** Anatomical Spec B28 points to Larry's and Bass under market-hog-only despite sow-only descriptions elsewhere. Put the approximate 140–220 lb live-weight requirement in the actual specification fields. Reconcile the owner-edited plan's aorta scope with the workbook's thoracic column. Preserve the boar/stag rule and identify any segregation exception as an owner decision.

5. **Preservation and slaughter process:** Anatomical Spec B31 incorrectly treats a short slaughter-to-preservation interval as automatically requiring our own crew. A trained supplier may preserve tissue immediately. Rated Candidates V9 proposes asking Piedmont to skip a stunning step; replace that proposed approach with documenting the existing humane process and having qualified plant staff and the inspector assess tissue suitability.

6. **Cost calculator:** D10, D11, D16, D21, D22, D23 and D24 contain explanatory prose stored as formulas. Propose a cost-per-accepted-specimen model with rejection losses, tissue purchase charges, clear labor units and comparisons of equivalent products. 7. **Filters and contradictory text:** Supplier Roster's filter is A4:T4; National Direct Suppliers' is A4:I4; Rated Candidates' is A5:V20, omitting seven newer rows and the last column. Propose full usable ranges with suitable treatment of non-data rows. Replace superseded current-field claims and retain history separately rather than leaving contradictory assertions appended in the same cell.

8. **Export guidance:** Reconcile inconsistent guidance across the instructions, anatomy and export tabs. Preserve documented company-approved pathways as such; do not infer universal legal rules from vendor marketing or assume a general research label settles the exact product's export eligibility. Identify the documentation and owner decisions needed.

## How to weigh the sources

- Match each fact to the exact legal entity, physical facility and activity.
- For inspection status, favor current official establishment records.
- For present managers, operations and weekly volumes, seek dated direct confirmation supported by current company material.
- Treat company service pages as company claims until independently or operationally verified.
- For export suitability, request documentation for the actual product, site, destination and intended use.
- Keep original dated reviews, responses and official findings separate.
- Distinguish original evidence from copies. Several listings repeating one old entry are not independent corroboration.
- Reconcile event dates and publication dates; a recently crawled page can contain old facts.
- Some Facebook pages were inaccessible directly during Beebop's pass. Indexed posts and mirrors must remain labeled as indirect evidence.
